Every Journey, Every Cabin
Four Ways to Fly

Full technical seat maps and configuration-by-aircraft breakdowns live on the Fleet page — this is what it actually feels like in each cabin.

🛏️
First Class
Private Suite · Door-to-Door
Fully enclosed suites with closing doors on the 777-9 and A350-1000, suite-style seating on Asian A350-900/787-8 routes. Fully flat beds, à la carte dining on demand, and check-in-to-cabin service from a dedicated First Class team.
💼
Business Class
Lie-Flat · Direct Aisle Access
1-2-1 full-flat seating with direct aisle access from every seat on long-haul flagship routes; 2-2-2 recliner-flat on the Asian network. Multi-course dining, noise-cancelling headsets, and priority everything — check-in, boarding, baggage.
🌿
Premium Economy
2-4-2 / 2-3-2 · Extra Room
A genuinely separate cabin, not a wider Economy seat — dedicated check-in and boarding lane, amenity kit, premium dining service, and a footrest-equipped seat with meaningfully more recline and legroom.
✈️
Economy
3-3-3 · 31" Pitch
In-seat power at every seat, adjustable headrests, and ANA's full entertainment library on every long-haul aircraft in the fleet — no exceptions, no legacy aircraft still running the old system.
On the Ground
Built to Move People Fast

A hub is only as good as its connections. Here's what makes the ground side of the Tokyo Mega Hub work.

6,000 m²
Flagship Lounge, T1
Three floors including a private First Class suite level with a teppanyaki counter and an 80-label sake and whisky bar. Open to Business, Premium Economy, and top-tier Mileage Club members across all three daily banks.
1-min
People Mover Frequency
A single line links T2 Main, both T2 satellites, T1 Asia, and T1 Domestic — plus biometric FastTrack boarding at every international gate, so a 45-minute connection is a real, guaranteed number, not a marketing one.
50 Airlines
One Hub, Every Alliance
Star Alliance, Oneworld, SkyTeam, and independent carriers like Emirates, Etihad, and WestJet all operate from dedicated terminal zones — see exactly who flies where on the Terminal Map.
