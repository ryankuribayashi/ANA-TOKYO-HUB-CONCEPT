Operations June 30, 2026
Tokyo Mega Hub Connects 12 Million Passengers in First Year of Full Operations
ANA's flagship hub at Narita International Airport has surpassed all first-year projections, welcoming over 12 million passengers across 115 routes on six continents. The milestone comes ahead of the hub's one-year anniversary and underscores Tokyo's emergence as Asia's premier long-haul transfer gateway — eclipsing Dubai, Singapore, and Seoul in connection volumes on the trans-Pacific corridor for the first time. Load factors across the five-aircraft fleet averaged 87.4%, with Business and First Class cabins consistently exceeding 91%.

Fleet June 18, 2026
ANA Boeing 777X Fleet Delivers 20% Fuel Burn Reduction Over Previous Generation
Six months of commercial revenue data confirm that ANA's Boeing 777-9 aircraft are achieving fuel burn reductions of 20% per seat compared to the retired 777-300ER fleet. The improvement — driven by the aircraft's composite wing flex design and GE9X engines — has allowed ANA to cut per-seat emissions on its highest-frequency routes including NRT–JFK, NRT–LAX, and NRT–SFO. ANA's Chief Operations Officer cited the figure as critical to the airline's 2030 carbon-neutral target for long-haul flying.

Routes June 5, 2026
Freedom Flights Program Expands to 31 European Cities via Eight Star Alliance Partners
ANA's innovative Freedom Flights program — which allows single-ticket connectivity from Tokyo to European cities via partner hubs — has expanded to 31 destinations in 22 countries. New additions include Dublin via Brussels, Lisbon via Zurich, Madrid via Geneva, and Budapest via Istanbul. Passengers benefit from full through-check baggage, ANA Mileage Club earning on all segments, and guaranteed connection times backed by ANA's 98.7% on-time performance record. Fares start from ¥142,000 in Economy and ¥485,000 in Business Class.

Awards May 29, 2026
ANA Ranked World's Most Punctual Long-Haul Airline for Third Year Running
OAG's 2026 Punctuality League has named ANA the world's most on-time long-haul carrier for the third consecutive year. Tokyo Mega Hub operations recorded a 98.7% on-time departure rate across all routes — a record for a hub of its size. The airline credits its three-wave bank schedule, 45-minute minimum connection standard, and precision ground operations.

Partnership May 15, 2026
Air Canada Upgrades KIX–Montréal to 4× Daily as Pacific Gateway Demand Surges
Air Canada has confirmed the expansion of its Osaka Kansai–Montréal Trudeau service from two to four daily departures, operating in full code-share with ANA under the NH 8007–8013 series. The move cements KIX's role as a premier second hub for westbound North American travelers, with seamless connections available to ANA's full Asian and Southeast Asian network. Partner fares are now available through ANA booking channels.
