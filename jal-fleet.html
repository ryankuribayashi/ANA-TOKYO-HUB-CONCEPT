JAL FLEET

Three Aircraft.
One Clear Strategy.

JAL runs a deliberately leaner widebody fleet than ANA's hub-scale build-out — no 777-9 flagship, no fifth aircraft type. Each of the three types has exactly one job: trunk long-haul, dual-role secondary/Asia flagship, or thin regional Asia.

Unlike ANA, which splits its A350-1000 into Premium and Main Trunk builds and tops its fleet with the 777-9, JAL runs one single A350-1000 configuration as its entire flagship — smaller and leaner than either ANA build, but with a genuine First Class cabin on every one of its twelve routes.
