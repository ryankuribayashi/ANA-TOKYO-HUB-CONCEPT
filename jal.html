The JAL Strategy

Fewer Routes,
Sharper Focus

Where ANA builds hub-scale reach with a five-aircraft fleet topped by the 777-9, JAL runs three aircraft types and 53 direct routes — matching ANA's frequency on the routes that matter most, and pulling ahead only where its own oneworld partners already hold ground: Hong Kong, 5× daily against ANA's 4, is JAL's clearest edge, the one city where Cathay Pacific's home advantage shows up directly in the schedule.

Everywhere else in secondary Greater China and at Manila and Taipei, JAL runs deliberately thinner than ANA — a supporting role inside the same hub, not a second hub-building push.
