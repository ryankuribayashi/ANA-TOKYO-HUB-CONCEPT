🎫 Booking & Fares
How do I book a flight?
Use the search widget on the homepage — enter your route, dates, and cabin, and you'll see every matching itinerary, including connections through the Tokyo Mega Hub, on the results page.
Can I change or cancel my booking?
Most fares allow date changes for a fee; our most flexible Business and First Class fares allow free changes and cancellation up to 24 hours before departure. Fare rules are shown at checkout before you pay.
What payment methods are accepted?
Major credit and debit cards, plus ANA Mileage Club miles for award bookings. Prices are shown in your selected currency at checkout using live conversion rates.
Do fares include checked baggage?
Premium Economy, Business, and First Class fares include checked baggage. Economy allowances vary by route and fare class — see Baggage below.
🧳 Baggage
What are the checked baggage allowances?
International Economy: 1 piece up to 23 kg. Premium Economy: 2 pieces up to 23 kg each. Business and First: 2–3 pieces up to 32 kg each, depending on route. Domestic connections follow ANA's standard domestic allowance.
What can I bring in carry-on?
One carry-on bag up to 10 kg plus one personal item, within standard international dimensions. Duty-free purchases made after security don't count against this limit.
What if my bag is delayed or damaged?
Report it at the baggage service desk in your arrival terminal before leaving the airport — every terminal zone at the Tokyo Mega Hub has one. On connecting itineraries booked as a single ticket, including Freedom Flights, your bag is checked through to your final destination.
🛫 Airport & Connections
What's the minimum connection time at the hub?
45 minutes, backed by a one-minute-frequency people mover linking every terminal zone and biometric FastTrack boarding at all international gates. See the Terminal Map for the full layout.
Which terminal do I depart from?
It depends on your route: T1 Asia for East/Southeast/South Asia, T2A for North America, T2B for Europe/Middle East/Africa/Oceania, and T2 Main for international low-cost carriers. Your terminal and gate are shown on your boarding pass and itinerary. Full breakdown, including every airline by terminal, is on the Terminal Map.
Do I need to reclear security when connecting?
Not within the same terminal complex. Connections between T1 and the T2 complex (T2 Main, T2A, T2B) use the people mover behind security, so you generally won't need to reclear — check your boarding pass, as this can vary for certain international-to-international routings.
How early should I arrive before my flight?
3 hours before international departure, 90 minutes before domestic. If you're connecting into an outbound flight on a single ticket, your minimum connection time already accounts for this.
🌐 Freedom Flights & Codeshares
What is the Freedom Flights program?
Single-ticket itineraries from Tokyo to cities ANA doesn't serve directly, routed through Star Alliance partner hubs — for example Dublin via Brussels, or Budapest via Istanbul. One booking, one baggage check-in, and full ANA Mileage Club earning across every segment. Full route list on the Destinations page.
Will I need to collect and recheck baggage on a Freedom Flight?
No — baggage is checked through to your final destination on the single ticket, the same as any other connecting itinerary.
Do I earn miles on partner-operated segments?
Yes, in full, on any Star Alliance partner segment booked on an ANA ticket. Codeshare or interline segments with Oneworld, SkyTeam, or independent carriers may earn under that carrier's own program instead — check the fare rules shown at booking.
⭐ ANA Mileage Club
How do I join ANA Mileage Club?
Membership is free — sign up during checkout or from your account settings. Miles from this and future bookings will apply automatically once your membership number is on file.
How do I redeem miles?
Award seats are bookable on ANA-operated flights and Star Alliance partner flights through the same search flow as a paid ticket — just switch to "Award Booking" before you search.
🤝 Special Assistance
Traveling with infants or young children?
Bassinets are available on request for long-haul flights (subject to aircraft type and availability), and children's amenity kits are provided in every cabin. Add details during booking or contact us in advance.
Wheelchair or mobility assistance?
Every terminal zone at the hub offers wheelchair assistance from check-in through to your connecting gate. Request this during booking or at least 48 hours before departure for guaranteed service.
Traveling with pets?
Small pets may travel in-cabin on select routes; larger animals travel as checked or cargo pets depending on aircraft type. Requirements vary by destination — contact us before booking to confirm eligibility.
